# Teacher workbook assumptions compared

The source explicitly says its limits were modeled on marketplaces, not extracted from EZiCart. A2–A8 require agreement before testing; A25–A37 also determine strict expectations. Do not silently treat those assumptions as approved changes.

| Assumption | Teacher rule | Assessment | Actual rule or scope difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1<br>Assumptions!C5 | The accounts on the Test Data sheet already exist and are Active or Approved (they are the accounts created in the Account Creation test set). Seller 3 is Pending Approval and Rider 3 is Suspended, for the negative cases. Reset the seeded products, stock and vouchers to their Test Data values before each module. | **Different** | Teacher demo accounts/products/vouchers are reference fixtures, not assumed present. No reseeding/reset was performed. |
| A2<br>Assumptions!C6 | Per order: at most 1 store voucher per store, plus 1 platform voucher, plus 1 shipping voucher. Selecting a second voucher of the same type replaces the first; it never stacks. | **Different** | One voucher per single-shop checkout; no store+platform+shipping stacking. |
| A3<br>Assumptions!C7 | Stock is deducted when the order is placed, for every payment method. It is restored when the order is cancelled (by buyer, seller or auto-cancel). For a Return-to-Sender or returned parcel, stock is restored when the seller confirms receipt. Stock can never go below 0. | **Partial** | Supported order stock deduction/cancel restore works; unpaid gateway reservation absent; returns require restock inspection. |
| A4<br>Assumptions!C8 | Vouchers apply to items already on sale. Minimum spend is checked against the price actually paid (the sale or flash price), not the original price. | **Missing** | No sale-price engine to combine with voucher. |
| A5<br>Assumptions!C9 | Sellers create flash sales for their own products in fixed time slots. When a product is in both a store sale and a flash sale, the flash price wins while flash quantity remains. Units beyond the remaining flash quantity in the same order are charged at the store sale price, or the regular price if there is no sale, and are shown on a separate line. | **Missing** | No flash allocation or fallback price split. |
| A6<br>Assumptions!C10 | A buyer can cancel until the seller marks the order Ready for Pickup; after that there is no cancel option at all. A seller can cancel until the parcel is Picked Up. Unpaid e-wallet or card orders auto-cancel exactly 1 hour after the order is placed. | **Partial** | Buyer cutoff at Ready for Pickup aligns; cancellation is seller-approved/24h, no one-hour unpaid gateway timer. |
| A7<br>Assumptions!C11 | A product has at most 2 variation types, with 1 to 20 options per type. Store categories have at most 2 levels (category and subcategory). A product can belong to up to 3 store categories. | **Different** | Shared category string and up to100 flat variation rows, not two axes/20 options/two category levels/three category memberships. |
| A8<br>Assumptions!C12 | Store vouchers, sale discounts, flash sales and bundle discounts are seller-funded. Platform vouchers and shipping vouchers are platform-funded and never reduce the seller's earnings. A platform voucher on a multi-store checkout is split across the store orders in proportion to each store's merchandise subtotal, rounded to the centavo, with any rounding remainder given to the largest store. When only some items in an order are refunded, the store voucher is split across the items in proportion to their price, and the refund is the item price minus its voucher share. | **Partial** | Sponsor ledger exists, but no sale/flash/bundle pricing, stacking, partial item refund allocation or new multi-store checkout. |
| A9<br>Assumptions!C13 | Commission is 5% of the item subtotal after seller-funded discounts. Shipping fees are not part of the seller's gross sales. All money is rounded to 2 decimals (half up) per order. | **Different** | Runtime/default10% global merchandise commission, not5%; configurable integer basis points. |
| A10<br>Assumptions!C14 | Buyer tabs: To Pay → To Ship → To Receive → Completed (or Cancelled, Return/Refund). Seller: New → Accepted → Packed → Ready for Pickup. Shipment: Ready for Pickup → Assigned → Picked Up → At Hub → Out for Delivery → Delivered (or Failed Delivery → Return to Sender → Returned to Sender). No status can be skipped or moved backwards except by a logged Logistics override. | **Partial** | Strict lifecycle exists with richer scanned hub/transfer/handoff stages and no generic override; no To Pay workflow. |
| A11<br>Assumptions!C15 | The buyer clicks Order Received, or the order completes automatically 7 days (168 hours) after Delivered. A return or refund can be requested only after Delivered, within those 7 days and before the order is Completed. | **Different** | Buyer receipt supported; no seven-day auto-completion; completed/late disputes remain allowed. |
| A12<br>Assumptions!C16 | A review is allowed only for Completed orders, within 30 days of completion, one review per item. Stars (1–5) are required; text is optional, up to 500 characters; up to 5 photos. It can be edited once within the 30 days. The seller can post one reply per review and edit it; the seller cannot delete or hide a buyer's review. | **Different** | One product review/order, completed required, text max1000; no photos/edit/reply/30-day window. |
| A13<br>Assumptions!C17 | Up to 3 delivery attempts. A reason from the list (Buyer not home, Wrong address, Refused) is required for every failed attempt. After the 3rd failed attempt, or immediately when the reason is Refused, the parcel is marked Return to Sender. | **Partial** | Three failure attempts supported; refused/third failure requires physical recovery instead of automatic RTS. |
| A14<br>Assumptions!C18 | A job offer expires after 60 seconds. A declined or expired offer goes to the next nearest eligible rider; after 3 riders decline or let it expire, the job goes to the Unassigned queue for manual assignment. A rider holds at most 3 active jobs. Only Active, Online riders receive offers. | **Different** | No Online or nearest-offer cascade;15-minute acceptance;10 active deliveries with verified cargo capacity. |
| A15<br>Assumptions!C19 | Earnings move from Pending to Available only when an order is Completed. Cancelled, returned and refunded amounts are excluded. A payout request must be at least ₱100.00 and no more than the Available balance, and only one payout request can be pending at a time. | **Different** | Completion+7day hold/funding/admin settlement; no100 minimum or one pending wallet withdrawal; order payout separate. |
| A16<br>Assumptions!C20 | Product, logo, banner, review, return, chat and proof-of-delivery images: JPG or PNG only (checked by file content, not just the extension), maximum 5 MB each. A product has 1 to 9 images. | **Different** | Image rules vary: most product/profile2MB+WebP; delivery4MB+PDF; chat5MB+documents; no review photos. |
| A17<br>Assumptions!C21 | Prices range from ₱1.00 to ₱1,000,000.00 with up to 2 decimal places. Stock and quantities are whole numbers from 0 to 99,999. | **Different** | Product name/price/stock bounds differ; buyer cart caps20; zero-image product accepted. |
| A18<br>Assumptions!C22 | In vacation mode, products stay visible but Add to Cart and Buy Now are disabled with an 'On vacation' label, and checkout of items already in carts is blocked. A seller that is not Approved can save products only as drafts and cannot publish. | **Partial** | Vacation blocks purchases, but pending sellers have no name-only Save Draft. |
| A19<br>Assumptions!C23 | Deleting a store category that still has products moves those products to 'Uncategorized'. Deleting a parent category also deletes its subcategories. A product or a variant with unfinished orders cannot be deleted; other deletes are soft deletes, so order history still shows the product's name, image and price. | **Different** | Archiving/compliance history and retired variants replace the specified unfinished-order block and store-category delete semantics. |
| A20<br>Assumptions!C24 | Shipping is charged per store, using the rate table on the Test Data sheet. Chargeable weight is the total actual weight of that store's items in the order, rounded up to the next whole kg. Rate changes apply only to checkouts started after the change. | **Different** | RuntimePHP50 flat; optional approved actual/volumetric bracket rates, not workbook whole-kg zone table. |
| A21<br>Assumptions!C25 | A voucher used on a cancelled order is returned to the buyer's wallet if it has not yet expired, and its usage count is reduced by 1. A voucher is not returned for a refunded (returned) order. | **Partial** | Funded cancellation restores usage after approval; post-delivery refund does not restore use. Legacy unbudgeted behavior needs separate review. |
| A22<br>Assumptions!C26 | Each role can open only its own pages; other roles get 403 or a redirect, for both pages and direct API requests. Users can open only their own records (orders, products, shipments, riders). A session expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. | **Partial** | Role/ownership tested; actual configured session lifetime120min, not30. Flutter/API client excluded from this web audit. |
| A23<br>Assumptions!C27 | Every order or shipment status change sends an in-app notification to each role involved, and to no one else. Emails use example.com and are read from the mail catcher. | **Partial** | Role notifications exist; email uses configured mail/queue, not a demonstrated catcher; exact every-recipient delivery not replayed. |
| A24<br>Assumptions!C28 | Riders remit the full COD collected to their logistics company by the end of the day. Only the logistics company can confirm a remittance. Any difference between collected and remitted COD is flagged on the reconciliation screen. | **Partial** | Independent exact remittance and batches exist; no end-of-day enforcement; discrepancy remains unverified. |
| A25<br>Assumptions!C29 | Store name 3–30 characters (unique, not case-sensitive, spaces trimmed). Store description up to 500. Store category name 1–50 (unique per store, not case-sensitive, trimmed). Product name 5–120. Product description 20–3,000. Note to seller up to 200. Chat message 1–1,000. Free-text reasons (cancel, reject, override, suspend) 10–300. HTML or script entered in any field is shown as plain text. | **Different** | Profile/product/chat/reason limits differ; see individual case rows and probes. |
| A26<br>Assumptions!C30 | 4 to 12 letters and digits, no spaces or symbols, saved in uppercase. Codes are unique across the whole platform. Buyers can type the code in any case. | **Different** | Uppercase/global code uniqueness, but max50 without4–12 alphanumeric check. |
| A27<br>Assumptions!C31 | Once any buyer has claimed a voucher, the seller can only extend its end date or raise its usage limit; all other fields are locked. Ending a voucher removes it from the wallets of buyers who have not used it; orders already placed keep their discount. | **Partial** | Pause/end blocks future use and retains order snapshot; no limited post-claim edit endpoint and claims remain retained. |
| A28<br>Assumptions!C32 | A product can be in only one store sale at a time; overlapping schedules are rejected. A running sale's discount can be edited and applies immediately to new orders; End Now ends it at once. Orders already placed keep the price paid. | **Missing** | No scheduled store-sale pricing. |
| A29<br>Assumptions!C33 | An alert is shown when a product's or variant's stock reaches 5 or fewer, once per crossing (it does not repeat until stock goes above 5 and drops again). | **Different** | Seller dashboard stock<10; buyer subscriptions use5; no specified seller/variant crossing alert. |
| A30<br>Assumptions!C34 | Up to 10 addresses per buyer. The default address cannot be deleted until another address is made default. Orders keep a copy of the address at order time, so editing or deleting an address does not change existing orders. | **Different** | No ten-address cap; default deletion automatically chooses replacement. Order snapshots align. |
| A31<br>Assumptions!C35 | Reason from the list, a description of 10–500 characters, and 1 to 5 photos (JPG or PNG, 5 MB each) are required. The seller must respond within 3 days or the request is auto-approved. A rejected request can be escalated to a dispute once, within 3 days. The refund goes to the original payment method; for COD orders, to the buyer's refund account. | **Different** | Admin-led dispute,20–2000 description, one optional evidence file; no seller3day/escalation chain or7day return limit. |
| A32<br>Assumptions!C36 | Delivered needs a photo (JPG or PNG) and the 6-digit delivery OTP shown in the buyer's app once the parcel is Out for Delivery. After 5 wrong OTP entries, OTP entry on that job is locked for 15 minutes. | **Different** | Photo/code required, but4MB/PDF allowance and refresh-based lock recovery differ from5MB/15min automatic unlock. |
| A33<br>Assumptions!C37 | ₱50.00 per successful delivery, ₱30.00 per completed pickup, ₱0.00 for a failed attempt. Pay is not reduced by COD. | **Different** | Approved cost component allocations at settlement, not fixedPHP30/PHP50 immediately; zero approved runtime tariffs. |
| A34<br>Assumptions!C38 | Auto-assign offers a job to the nearest Online, Active rider of the same company with fewer than 3 active jobs. Logistics can reassign a job only before it is Picked Up. Suspending a rider returns their not-yet-picked-up jobs to the Unassigned queue; parcels already picked up stay with that rider. | **Different** | Manual area-based assignment and custody recovery exceptions; no nearest-online auto-routing. |
| A35<br>Assumptions!C39 | Every manual status override, rider approval or suspension, rate change and remittance confirmation is logged with user, date and time, old value, new value and reason. Log entries cannot be edited or deleted from the UI. | **Partial** | Actors/before-after/reasons logged for existing actions, but manual arbitrary overrides do not exist. |
| A36<br>Assumptions!C40 | Reports export as CSV and XLSX. Exported rows and totals equal the on-screen rows and totals for the same filter. | **Partial** | AnalyticsCSV/XLSX/PDF exists; dedicated wallet/remittance/shipping statementsCSV only. Screen/report recognition scopes vary. |
| A37<br>Assumptions!C41 | Every create or submit action (Publish, Place Order, Pay, Request Payout, Accept job, Remit, Confirm) is idempotent: double-clicking, pressing Enter twice, refreshing or pressing Back and resubmitting never creates a second record or a second charge. | **Partial** | Checkout/ledger/claim/scan/payout paths have locks/unique keys; product create duplicates demonstrated. Not every create endpoint is idempotent. |
